# `<Question>`: research

> Lines in quotes are guidance. Replace each with content, then delete the guidance. Writing rules: `writing-style`. Method for external sources: `external-research`.

## Question

> One sentence. State the decision it feeds.

## Verdict

> Up to 10 lines: the answer, the confidence, which docs changed because of it, and which options were dropped and why.

## Method

> What was searched, which sources count as primary, the date of the sweep, and the privacy rule applied (no real personal or financial data in any query).

## Findings

> One entry per finding. Each has a claim, a link to the primary source, and either a verbatim quote under 25 words checked against that source or a paraphrase marked "paraphrase". Label each claim as fact (the source states it) or inference (you conclude it).

## Contradictions

> Sources that disagree, what each says at its own strength, and which one you weight and why.

## What was not checked

> Sources not reached, claims taken from a single source, features documented but not tested.

## Recommendation

> The action, the evidence it rests on (at least two independent sources), and the smallest test that would change your mind.
