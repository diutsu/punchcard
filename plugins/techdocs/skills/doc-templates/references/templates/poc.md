> Lines in quotes are guidance. Replace each with content, then delete the guidance. Keep the brief to one page. Write sections 1 to 6 before any code; fill section 7 when the proof of concept ends.

## 1. Question and goals

> One sentence: the question this answers and the decision it feeds. Then the goals, at most three, each one checkable.

## 2. Entry criteria

> What must be true before work starts: inputs available, access granted, the unknown named. If one is missing, the proof of concept does not start.

## 3. Scope and time box

> What is built, what is faked or skipped, and what is out of scope. The time box in hours or days. Where the code lives (branch or worktree), marked as throwaway.
